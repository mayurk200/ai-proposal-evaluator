## Project/Product Name
Stoma Sense: AI-Driven Remote Sensing, Moisture, Growth and Yield Intelligence for Sugarcane in Maharashtra
## Solution Synopsis
Stoma Sense is an integrated GEOAI platform that helps farmers track crop growth and optimise sugarcane harvest quality and productivity. It combines Sentinel-1/2 time-series, deep-learning cloud-free reconstruction, cane-moisture samples, yield history, irrigation telemetry and weather data to deliver continuous, plot-level intelligence. Its U-Net GAN engine reconstructs clean multispectral bands even during monsoon cloud cover, ensuring uninterrupted NDVI, NDRE, NDWI, HYDRO+, growth, Potential Growth Index (PGI), weed and stress indicators.
A key differentiator is the cane-moisture model, which learns relationships between spectral indices, rainfall–ET balance, soil/climate/variety/irrigation factors and lab-measured moisture %, enabling accurate moisture estimation and its impact on TCH yield. PGI benchmarks each plot’s spectral trajectory against historical cohorts to detect stagnation or hidden stress that NDVI may miss.
Building on this foundation, Stoma Sense forecasts moisture deficit, weed outbreaks, growth anomalies and TCH yield using ML models with SHAP-based explainability. Advisories are delivered via a simple field app, while managers use a web dashboard for planning and monitoring. The modular, cloud-native system scales across mills and districts and adapts to other irrigated crops, providing a blueprint for statewide digital agriculture transformation.

## Explain the technology you are using in detail
Stoma Sense is built on a GEOAI architecture that integrates satellite analytics, agronomic modeling and cloud-native automation into a single operational pipeline. The system runs a multi-stage chain—ingestion of Sentinel-1/2 imagery, atmospheric correction, tiling, cloud-reconstruction inference, spectral index computation and plot-level aggregation—all executed on a scalable cloud environment to support continuous monitoring across large command areas.
Its deep-learning reconstruction module, trained on paired cloudy and cloud-free imagery, restores per-band spectral information, ensuring temporal continuity even when optical data is obstructed. Pixel-level values are transformed into agronomic indicators using rule-based and ML methods. Plot boundaries, variety maps and irrigation metadata contextualize these signals so that predictions reflect real management conditions. Data fusion of radar, optical, moisture samples, telemetry and weather feeds the cane-moisture model, growth segmentation and TCH forecasting.
The platform uses containerised microservices for preprocessing, modeling and alert generation, with autoscaling and asynchronous orchestration so each new satellite acquisition triggers updated analytics and advisories.
Stoma Sense’s mobile and web layers access results via secure APIs, enabling real-time advisories, field reporting and zone-level dashboards without exposing any personal data.

## Please explain your pricing strategy. Additionally, describe how affordable your
## solution will be for farmers and how you have assessed their ability to pay
Stoma Sense uses a subscription model of ₹59 per acre per month (₹708 per acre annually), keeping costs low for small farmers while enabling large-scale adoption through FPOs, cooperatives, mills, and government programs.
Affordability was benchmarked against Maharashtra sugarcane economics, where yields typically range from 80–100 tonnes per hectare (H32–40 tonnes per acre). At the 2025–26 FRP of ₹3,550 per tonne, farmers earn roughly ₹1.1–₹1.4 lakh per acre annually, with some reporting up to ₹3 lakh.
Against this, the annual cost of ₹708 per acre is just 0.57% of gross revenue—far below typical losses from suboptimal irrigation, delayed stress detection, or poor harvest timing. In a 14,000-hectare field test, the platform delivered 100× ROI through avoidable-loss savings even after service costs.
Affordability is especially important given current FRP delays in Maharashtra, where only ~53% of dues were paid by November 2025 and arrears exceeded ₹1,980 crore. With farmers facing cash-flow uncertainty, a low-cost digital advisory that stabilizes yields and reduces risk becomes critical. The subscription remains below the cost of a single irrigation cycle, ensuring adoption even in delayed-payment years.
Because Stoma Sense has no hardware dependency, low marginal cost, and scalable GEOAI pipelines, the pricing model remains sustainable for district- and state-level expansion while addressing a TAM of ₹259.84 crore for sugarcane and ₹3,026 crore across major crops.

From a sustainability perspective, the platform enables efficient water use, early stress detection, and optimized input application, directly supporting Maharashtra’s priorities around water management, climate resilience, and sustainable agricultural growth. The pilot provides a replicable blueprint for scalable, AI-driven agricultural transformation across the state.
